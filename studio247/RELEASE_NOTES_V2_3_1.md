# Creator Studio v2.3.1

- Removed unused legacy listener components containing dead routes and obsolete Experience language.
- Preserves current Projects, Commissions, Deliverables, Contracts, Earnings, Certificates and notification workflows.

## v2.3.2 — Deliverable revision workflow
- Creator workspace now surfaces revision requests and staff instructions.
- Revised deliverables can be edited and resubmitted.
- Submitted deliverables are locked until a revision is requested.
- Revision resubmission returns the deliverable to review and notifies Enterprise.
